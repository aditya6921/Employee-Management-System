Employee Management & Attendance System

A simple employee management and attendance system designed for local and small-to-medium-sized businesses that need an easier way to manage employee records, attendance, working hours, and additional work.

📌 Problem

Many small businesses still manage employee information and attendance using paper records or manual calculations.

For example, an owner may need to keep track of:

Which employees are present or absent each day
How many days an employee has worked
Additional working hours
Extra work performed by an employee
Additional payment for extra work
Monthly attendance and work records

When these records are maintained manually, calculating everything at the end of the month can become:

Time-consuming
Error-prone
Difficult to maintain
Difficult to verify
Frustrating for both the owner and employees

There is also a lack of transparency when records are maintained only on paper.

💡 Idea Behind the Project

This project was created to provide a simple digital alternative to manual employee record keeping.

The goal is not to build a complex enterprise HR system, but to create something practical for local businesses and small teams where the owner needs a straightforward way to keep track of employees and their attendance.

The system aims to make daily record keeping easier and reduce the amount of manual calculation required at the end of the month.

🚀 Features

The system is being developed incrementally, with functionality added over multiple development stages.

Employee Management
Maintain employee records
Store employee-related information
Manage employee data digitally instead of relying on paper records
Attendance Management
Track employee attendance
Maintain daily attendance records
Keep historical attendance information
Work & Extra Work Tracking
Record working information
Track additional work or working hours
Maintain records that can be used for additional payment calculations
Data Management
Store employee-related data
Maintain records between application sessions
Organize employee and attendance information
